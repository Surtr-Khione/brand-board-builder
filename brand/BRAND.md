## 1. The short version

**What BrandMD is:** a brand tool that scores your brand, builds the full brand system, and checks everything you
publish against it.

**Who it is for:** founders and small teams who cannot afford an agency and will not settle for a logo and two colors.

**The belief:** branding has been sold as taste. It can be measured.

**The enemy:** Guesswork. The rebrand chosen by whoever spoke last in the meeting, the moodboard nobody can defend,
the statistic nobody can source.

**The promise:** your brand, measured and made usable, in minutes.

**Tagline (keep):** Give your brand gravity.

**One-liner:** Most founders guess at their brand. BrandMD scores it, builds the full system, and checks everything
you publish against it, so people remember you and AI describes you right.

## 3. Strategy

**Purpose.** Replace guesswork with evidence in how brands get built.

**Mission.** Give every founder the brand system big companies pay agencies for, built from evidence, in minutes,
and keep it working after launch.

**Vision.** Every brand can be read clearly, by people and by machines.

**Values** ("We X, even when Y"):
- **We show the working,** even when the honest answer is "we don't know yet."
- **We give the real diagnosis free,** even when a teaser would sell more upgrades.
- **We measure,** even when taste is louder in the room.
- **We make it usable,** even when a 60-page PDF would look more impressive.

**The belief statement.** We believe the branding industry is wrong to sell taste as if it were truth. A brand is a
set of signals people and machines either remember or don't, and that can be checked.

## 6. Personality and voice

| We are | Not |
|---|---|
| Precise | Clinical |
| Confident | Cocky |
| Generous | Salesy |
| Curious | Academic |

**Tone map.**
- Homepage and ads: confident, short, a number in the first line when possible.
- Product UI: plain and instructive; say what happened and what to do next.
- Scores and audits: honest, specific, never cruel. Name the weakest signal and the fix.
- Journal: the BrandMD Desk voice (`content-network/sites/brandmd/brand/voice.md`): a science reporter who loves brands.
- Errors and empty states: calm, one line, one action.

**Lexicon.** Use: gravity, signal, score, board, chart, roadmap, evidence, the working, recognized, remembered,
distinctive, on-brand, drift. Never: synergy, elevate, unlock, leverage, seamless, world-class, revolutionary,
game-changer, "AI-powered" as a selling point, "studies show".

**Style rules.**
- **No em dashes or en dashes.** The live site has 22 on the homepage alone; replace with commas, colons or periods.
- American spelling ("color").
- Sentence case for headings. Numbers as numerals.
- One CTA wording per action across the whole product (see §8).

**Sample (target).** "Your brand has a number. Paste your URL and see it in under a minute, with the three fixes that
would raise it most. Free, no signup."

### Color tokens

| Token | Hex | Role | Contrast notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Night | `#000000` | App hero and immersive moments only | |
| Ink | `#0B0B0C` | Dark surfaces, Journal masthead, tool cards | Paper on Ink 18.1:1 |
| Paper | `#F5F5F7` | Light surfaces, reading pages | |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards on Paper, forms | |
| Titanium | `#8E8E93` | Labels and secondary text **on dark only** | 6.4:1 on Night; **fails on Paper (3.0:1)** |
| Graphite | `#6E6E73` | Labels and secondary text **on light** | 4.7:1 on Paper |
| Signal Blue | `#0071E3` | **The one accent:** primary buttons, meters, the photographic signal | White on Signal Blue 4.7:1; as text on Night only 4.5:1 (large only) |
| Link Blue (light) | `#0066CC` | Text links on Paper and White | 5.1:1 on Paper |
| Link Blue (dark) | `#2997FF` | Text links on Night and Ink | 7.0:1 on Night |

Rules: neutrals do about 90% of the work. Signal Blue appears on the primary action and on data, never as
decoration or as a second headline color on the same screen. Brand colors of other companies appear only inside
their own library records and in Journal photographs where they are the subject.

### Typography

- **Inter only.** Display: Inter Display cuts, weight 700 to 800, tracking -0.02 to -0.035em, line-height 1.02 to
  1.1. Text: Inter 400/500/600, 17 to 18px body on marketing pages, 18 to 19px in the Journal, line-height 1.5 to 1.6.
- Scale: 1.25 for app and marketing, 1.333 for the Journal. One display jump per view.
- Caps labels: 12 to 13px, weight 600, tracking +0.08 to +0.12em, Titanium on dark, Graphite on light.
- Tabular numerals for every score, count and table.
- **Brand pages** currently use a serif display for brand names (it reads as each brand's own world). Keep it there
  only; it is the library's device, not BrandMD's voice.

### Shape, space and layout

- Radius family: **8** (inputs, chips), **14** (cards), **pill** (buttons). One family everywhere; today the app uses
  16 and the Journal uses hard edges and 8.
- Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128. Section padding 96 desktop, 64 mobile.
- Containers: 1200 for app pages, 1180 for the Journal front, 680 to 720 for reading columns.
- **Left-aligned by default.** Center only the hero question and single-action closers. The homepage today centers
  every section.
- Hairlines (1px, 8% to 12% opacity) separate; shadows are rare and tinted.

### The shared frame (what makes the app and the Journal one brand)

- **One nav** on every surface: the orbit mark, "BrandMD", the same items in the same order, and one primary
  button. Items: Score, Build, Check, Library, Research, Journal. Button: **Get your score**.
- **One footer** with the same links.
- **One CTA wording per action:** "Get your score" (analyzer), "Build your brand" (Builder or Founder Mode),
  "Check a draft" (Brand Check). Retire "Chart your brand", "Get My Score" and "Get Your Score: Free".

## 9. Imagery: Signal photography

**The idea.** The world in black and white, and one signal in color. It is BrandMD's thesis made visible: most of
what a person sees is noise, and a strong brand is the one signal that gets through. Every photograph says it
without a caption.

**Two uses of the signal.**
- **On BrandMD's own surfaces** (homepage, ads, app empty states, social): the signal is **Signal Blue**, recolored
  from one object in the frame (a swatch, a sign, a light). It becomes a distinctive asset: blue in a grey world
  means BrandMD.
- **In the Journal and the library** (Case Files, Brand Battles, brand pages): the signal is **the subject brand's
  own color** (Tropicana's orange, Coca-Cola's red), because that color is the lesson.

**Treatment spec** (`~/.claude/skills/brand-imagery/scripts/treat.py`):
- Black and white with a gentle S-curve (contrast 1.1 to 1.2), true blacks allowed, no sepia, no blue cast.
- One signal hue only, covering **3% to 25%** of the frame. Above 30% the image stops reading as black and white;
  below 3% the accent reads as noise.
- Feathered mask edge (4 to 8px), no hard cut-outs.
- Crops: 3:2 for leads, 4:5 for social and cards, 16:9 for heroes, 1:1 for library tiles.
- Optional fine grain (0.02 to 0.04) for the Journal only.

**Subjects.** Brands in the wild and the work of making them: signage, shelves, packaging, storefronts at night,
swatches, type specimens, printed proofs, screens with design work, city streets full of logos. Hands at work are
fine. **Faces are not the hero.** No identifiable people without a release; never AI-generated people.

**Sources (in order).** Our own photography → free licensed (CC0 first, then Pexels / Unsplash, then CC BY with
credit; never CC BY-SA or NC, because the treatment is a derivative) → Envato Elements (licensed per project,
recorded) → AI generation (objects and places only, from the brand-imagery prompt template, labeled internally as AI).
Every image is logged in `credits.json` with its license.

**Don't.** Stock handshakes, smiling teams at laptops, purple gradients, glowing brains, robots, "AI" imagery,
colorized photos with more than one hue, a signal that is not the subject.

The first treated set lives in `brand/imagery/treated/` (CC0 sources, credits in `brand/imagery/src/credits.json`).

photo moodboard and a photo-backed closing CTA) was built on July 2, 2026 (commit `866be1d`) on the branch

are hotlinks to the brands' own CDNs.

**Plan.** Port the gallery and image sections by hand from that commit (about 150 lines, each section already hides
itself without images); switch logos to `logo_url`; cache hero images into our own storage; and for the 69 brands
without photos, fill the gaps with **Signal photography of the brand in the wild** (signage, shelves, packaging) from
licensed sources, treated so the brand's own color is the signal.
