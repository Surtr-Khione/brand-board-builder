# Gravity phrases

*BrandMD's space language, v1 (2026-11-06). The metaphor has one job: make a brand's strength feel like a physical
force a founder can increase. Use it where it clarifies (scores, launches, momentum) and drop it where it would
confuse (pricing, legal, support). No em dashes. One space phrase per screen is plenty.*

## The six Gravity tiers

Each score lands in one tier. The tier name is the badge; the headline is what the score screen says; the next move
is the first fix from the roadmap.

| Score | Tier | Headline | What it means | Next move |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 19 | **Grounded** | Your brand hasn't left the launchpad. | The basics aren't written down yet: who it's for, what it stands for, how it sounds. | Start with the mission and the audience. Everything else orbits those two. |
| 20 to 39 | **Lift-off** | You're off the ground. Now hold a course. | Some signals are set, but they don't add up to one brand yet. | Pick the archetype and name the enemy. A brand needs a direction, not just thrust. |
| 40 to 59 | **In orbit** | People can see you. Not all of them remember you. | The brand is defined but thin in places a buyer notices: voice, colors, proof. | Deepen the voice and lock the color system. Repetition is how orbits become habits. |
| 60 to 74 | **Gravity well** | You're starting to pull people in. | A real brand with a few gaps. Most well-known brands in our library start here. | Close the top roadmap item, then check new drafts against the board. |
| 75 to 89 | **Escape velocity** | Your brand moves on its own momentum. | Clear, consistent, distinctive. The job now is protection from drift. | Turn on Drift Watch and run Brand Check before anything ships. |
| 90 to 100 | **Out of this world** | A brand people orbit. | Every core signal is present and defined. Very few brands get here. | Guard the distinctive assets. Change slowly and on purpose. |

Copy rules for tiers: never mock a low score ("Grounded" is a starting point, not an insult); always pair the tier
with the next move; never promise that a higher tier means more sales (the score measures how complete and defined
a brand is, not revenue).

## Words to use and to avoid

Use: gravity, orbit, pull, signal, launch, lift-off, escape velocity, mission control, launchpad, course, drift.
Avoid: rocket emoji, "to the moon" (crypto connotation), "blast off", "astronomical growth", "galaxy brain",
aliens, anything that promises growth from a score.
